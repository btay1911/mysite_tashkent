Heart of Modern Uzbekistan

Tashkent is the capital city of Uzbekistan and the largest city in Central Asia, home to over two million people. Rebuilt after a devastating 1966 earthquake, it grew into a striking mix of wide Soviet-era boulevards, gleaming modern towers, and ornate Islamic architecture. By day, visitors wander through bustling bazaars like Chorsu, ride one of the most beautifully decorated metro systems in the world, and sample fragrant plov, the national dish of rice, meat, and carrots simmered for hours. By night, the city comes alive with rooftop restaurants, fountains, and a nightlife scene that blends East and West. Whether you're drawn by its history, its food, or its fast-growing skyline, Tashkent offers a fascinating window into modern Uzbekistan.
